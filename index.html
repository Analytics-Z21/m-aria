Questions

M Aria, explained

The answers below come from the project’s own published material, including its Advertising and Sales Permit.

M Aria sits in Sentul, Kuala Lumpur, about 5 km from the KL city centre. The DUKE Highway is 1.5 km away, a Rapid KL bus stop is 350 m away, and KTM Kampung Batu is 2.8 km away. LRT Sentul and MRT2 Kampung Batu are both 3.8 km away.

M Aria is freehold. The land tenure is stated as freehold on the project’s Advertising and Sales Permit.

Two layouts are offered:

  • Type A — 3 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms, 800 sq ft
  • Type B — 4 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms, 950 sq ft

Each unit comes with 2 to 4 car park bays.

The Advertising and Sales Permit states a selling price of RM635,000 (minimum) to RM750,000 (maximum). A price per layout is not published; request the current price list and our team will send it to you.

Residensi M Aria comprises 606 apartment units. The expected date of completion stated on the permit is January 2030.

58 urban resort facilities across the LG Floor, First Floor, Level 7 and Level 8. They include a swimming pool, kids pool, jacuzzi, sun deck, gym, games room, co-working space, BBQ terrace, community plaza, jogging trail, reflexology trails, a multipurpose hall that doubles as a pickleball court, a kids’ playground, taska and tadika, EV charging bays, a parcel room, surau, and multi-tier security.

The M Aria Sales Gallery is at Lot No. 468-B (B), Block B, Rivercity, Jalan Ipoh, 51200 Kuala Lumpur.

It opens daily: Monday to Friday 9.30am to 6.00pm, Saturday and Sunday 10.00am to 6.00pm. Call 03 9212 0438 or register for a visit and our team will confirm your slot.

Yes. M Aria is GreenRE Silver certified. The development also includes EV charging bays and a bicycle docking bay.
